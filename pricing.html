Questions

Good to know

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. CFIs get 30 days free without entering a card, and schools are free during early access.

Do my students pay anything?

No. The instructor's plan covers unlimited students, and they join free on the app or the web.

Can I subscribe in the iPhone app?

Yes. You can subscribe in the app through the App Store or on the web. Either way, the same plan unlocks PartFive everywhere you sign in.

What happens if I cancel?

Your logbook, schedule and preflight checks stay free, and you can export all your data at any time.
