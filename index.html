About

Purpose-built for aviation

Why "PartFive"

The name comes from 14 CFR Part 5 — the FAA's framework for Safety Management Systems. Today, Part 5 is formally required only for the largest aviation operators. But ICAO recommends SMS for every training organization, insurance carriers increasingly favor it, and the regulatory direction is clear: SMS is the future of every aviation operation.

PartFive exists to make that future accessible now. Part 5-aligned safety management — for the smaller operators, flight training organizations, and independent instructors who aren't required to do it, but should, and increasingly want to.

Our mission

PartFive was built by a working aviation safety professional, in a real flight training operation, doing real safety work — not by a software company guessing what aviation needs. The goal: raise the floor of safety management across the operators who don't have access to enterprise-grade tools, and make doing the right thing easier than not doing it.

For flight training organizations, that means audit-ready SOPs, manageable SMS workflows, and a single record of truth. For independent instructors, that means keeping track of every student's progress, every endorsement, and the safety trends in your own operation — the same discipline the big organizations have, in a tool built for one CFI. Every feature in PartFive comes from a real operational need. Every regulatory reference is from work actually being done in the field.

Platform capabilities

Everything your operation needs

Eight integrated modules — designed around how aviation organizations actually work.

Safety Management

AI-assisted SMS manual authoring across all four ICAO Annex 19 pillars. SOP library with versioning and acknowledgement tracking. Safety reports with full investigation and corrective action workflows, safety meetings, and bulletins.

Curriculum & Training

AI-assisted FAA-compliant syllabus generation — Private Pilot through CFI, Part 61 and Part 141. Lesson plans with AC 61-65K endorsement checkpoints pre-mapped, stage checks, and end-of-course workflow through graduation.

Pre-flight Risk Assessment

PAVE-based risk model with altitude scoring, CFI sign-off workflow, and regulatory endorsement gating — so no student flies without the right review.

Endorsements

All 30 FAA endorsement templates from AC 61-65K, frozen snapshots, revocation lifecycle, and personal-logbook reminder per §61.51.

Flight Operations

Scheduling, flight log, stage checks, standardization flights, and companion-pilot tracking — the operational record your auditors will ask for.

Student Lifecycle

Roster, progress tracking, graduation certificates, and student→CFI promotion with full audit trail — from enrollment through career advancement.

Fleet Management

Aircraft tracking with airworthiness inspections, annual and 100-hour due dates, ELT battery, registration expiration, and base location.

Roles & Authority

Safety Officer, Admin, Chief CFI, Assistant Chief CFI (per FAR 141.36), CFI, Independent CFI, Student. RLS-enforced data isolation. Audit trail on every regulated action.
