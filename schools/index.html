Questions

Good to know

Does it work for Part 141 and Part 61 schools?

Yes, both. Part 141 schools also get chief and assistant chief instructor roles and stage checks.

Is there a mobile app for our instructors?

The school side runs in any web browser, including on a phone or tablet. The iPhone and iPad app is for independent instructors today.

Can our instructors and students each have their own login?

Yes. Everyone gets their own account with the right access for their role, from student to chief instructor.

How do we get our people on?

Share an invite code. Instructors and students join in a minute, and you approve each one.
