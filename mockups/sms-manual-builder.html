1.1 — Safety Policy Statement
Skyway Aviation is committed to achieving the highest practicable level of safety in all flight training operations. This commitment is reflected in our systematic approach to hazard identification, risk assessment, and corrective action — and in the allocation of organizational resources necessary to support effective safety management.

All personnel are responsible for maintaining awareness of safety hazards within their area of operation, reporting hazards and incidents through established channels, and participating actively in safety improvement initiatives.

1.3 — Safety Performance Targets
Safety performance is measured against the following indicators, reviewed quarterly by the Safety Officer and reported to the Accountable Executive:

· SPT-1: Zero runway incursions attributable to Skyway Aviation personnel
· SPT-2: 100% completion of required safety training within 30 days of assignment
· SPT-3: All open hazard reports resolved or formally accepted within 60 days
· SPT-4: Pre-flight Risk Assessment completion rate ≥ 95% for solo student flights
